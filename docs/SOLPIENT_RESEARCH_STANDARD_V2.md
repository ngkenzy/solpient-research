# Solpient Research Standard v2

Governing question: Does this company offer a sufficiently attractive combination of business quality, valuation, expected return, and downside protection to justify owning it instead of a broad-market index?

V2 keeps one report structure across companies while allowing the metric set to adapt to the business model. The report covers investment thesis, business quality, financial quality, fundamental scorecard, competitive position, valuation, historical valuation, expected return, risk analysis, Buffett/Lynch lenses, and a decision dashboard plus five-part conclusion.

Metrics use three layers: universal core, industry module, and company-specific thesis variables. Not-applicable is a first-class state; the system must not force a ratio onto an industry where it is economically misleading.

The private backend stores historical metrics, valuation history, peer sets, peer snapshots, capital allocation history, freshness, update plans, checklist rules/results, and the additional v2 report sections.

The original conservative investor checklist is preserved separately. PEG, debt/equity, payout ratio, liquidity ratios, margins, ROE and similar thresholds are contextual checks rather than hard rejection rules.

The update planner does not rewrite every report every day. Market data can refresh daily. Large price moves trigger valuation review. New normalized fundamentals trigger a fundamentals refresh. New material filings trigger deep research refresh. If nothing material changed, no new research version is created.
